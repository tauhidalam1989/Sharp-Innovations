Cybersecurity GRC Services

Sharp Innovations provides comprehensive Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) services to help organizations align cybersecurity with regulatory requirements, mitigate risks, and enhance overall security posture. Our GRC services ensure that your organization remains compliant, secure, and resilient against evolving cyber threats.

Our Key Services

  • Regulatory Compliance Management:
    • Navigating the complex landscape of regulatory requirements can be challenging. Sharp Innovations ensures that your organization remains compliant with industry standards and regulations, including:

    • NIST (National Institute of Standards and Technology)
    • ISO 27001 (International Organization for Standardization - Information Security)
    • PCI-DSS (Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard)
    • NCA - National Cyber Security Authority
    • GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)
    • SAMA - Saudi Arabia Monetary Authority
    • Gap assessments to identify compliance deficiencies
    • Compliance roadmap development
    • Policy alignment with regulatory frameworks
    • Assistance in audits and regulatory reporting
    • Ongoing compliance monitoring and updates
  • Risk Management Framework (RMF) Implementation:

    • Sharp Innovations provides end-to-end implementation of the Risk Management Framework (RMF) as per NIST SP 800-37. Our structured approach enhances your organization’s security posture by identifying, assessing, and mitigating risks effectively.

    • Risk Assessment & Categorization – Identifying critical assets and threat vectors
    • Control Selection & Implementation – Mapping appropriate security controls
    • Security Assessment & Authorization – Ensuring controls meet security requirements
    • Continuous Monitoring & Improvement – Real-time threat detection and mitigation
  • Policy & Procedure Development:

    • A strong cybersecurity program requires well-defined policies and procedures. Sharp Innovations develops customized security policies aligned with industry best practices and regulatory requirements.

    • Security policies covering access control, incident response, and data protection
    • Standard operating procedures (SOPs) for security teams
    • Security awareness and training guidelines for employees
    • Governance models ensuring accountability and compliance
  • Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM):

    • Managing risks from third-party vendors is crucial to safeguarding your organization’s sensitive data. Sharp Innovations provides a structured approach to assessing and mitigating third-party risks.

    • Vendor risk assessments and due diligence
    • Contract review and security compliance checks
    • Continuous monitoring of third-party security postures
    • Incident response planning for third-party breaches
  • Continuous Security Audits & Reporting:

    • Proactive security auditing is essential for maintaining compliance and mitigating risks before they escalate. Sharp Innovations conducts thorough security audits and provides detailed reporting to help organizations strengthen their cybersecurity framework.
