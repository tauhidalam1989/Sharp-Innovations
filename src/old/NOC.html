Superior Monitoring Network Operations Center (NOC) Service

Sharp Innovations provides a cutting-edge Network Operations Center (NOC) service designed to ensure high availability, security, and efficiency in managing network infrastructures. Our NOC service encompasses 24/7 network monitoring, proactive issue resolution, security management, performance optimization, and compliance adherence.



We Provide End-to-End NOC Service

  • NOC Consulting: Expert guidance to design and implement NOC solutions tailored to your needs.
  • Seamless Onboarding: Smooth transition and integration of your network into our NOC services.
  • Managed Network Services: Comprehensive management of your network infrastructure.
  • Continuous Improvement: Ongoing optimization and updates to ensure peak performance.

Operational Activities

Our NOC service is structured around the following key operational activities:

  • Network Monitoring & Incident Management:
    • Continuous monitoring of network performance, uptime, and health.
    • Proactive identification of anomalies and potential issues.
    • Automatic alerting and incident escalation.
    • Rapid troubleshooting and remediation of network failures.
  • Performance Optimization:
    • Real-time network traffic analysis.
    • Bandwidth and latency monitoring.
    • Load balancing and congestion management.
    • Tuning network parameters for optimal performance.
  • Security & Compliance Management:
    • Intrusion detection and prevention system (IDS/IPS) monitoring.
    • Firewall and endpoint security management.
    • Threat intelligence and vulnerability assessment.
    • Compliance enforcement (NCA, ISO 27001, NIST, etc.).
  • Patch & Configuration Management:
    • Regular firmware and software updates.
    • Configuration audits and policy enforcement.
    • Change management to minimize network disruptions.
  • Reporting & Analytics:
    • Custom dashboard for real-time insights.
    • Monthly performance reports.
    • Root cause analysis for recurring issues.
    • Predictive analytics for capacity planning.

Architecture of NOC Service

Our NOC is built on a robust and scalable architecture to ensure seamless operations:

  • Multi-Tiered Architecture:
    • Monitoring Layer: Collects and aggregates data from various network elements.
    • Analysis Layer: Uses AI-driven analytics and machine learning for anomaly detection.
    • Resolution Layer: Implements automated remediation, manual intervention, and escalation policies.
    • Reporting & Dashboard Layer: Provides real-time visualization and insights.
  • Cloud-Enabled & On-Premises Capabilities:
    • Hybrid cloud deployment for flexibility and scalability.
    • Secure VPN and encrypted communication channels.
    • Distributed NOC centers for redundancy and disaster recovery.
  • Integration with ITSM & SIEM:
    • Seamless integration with IT Service Management (ITSM) platforms like ServiceNow and Jira.
    • Security Information and Event Management (SIEM) tools for proactive threat hunting.

Our NOC Team Structure

A well-defined team ensures the efficiency of our NOC services:

  • Level 1 (L1) - Network Monitoring Engineers:
    • First point of contact for alerts and incidents.
    • Basic troubleshooting and escalation to L2.
  • Level 2 (L2) - Network Support Specialists:
    • Advanced troubleshooting and root cause analysis.
    • Patch management and security enforcement.
  • Level 3 (L3) - Network Architects & Security Analysts:
    • Infrastructure design and complex problem resolution.
    • Security threat mitigation and forensic analysis.
  • NOC Manager & Compliance Officers:
    • Oversight of NOC operations and strategy.
    • Ensuring adherence to security and regulatory compliance.

  • Fast-Track Your Growth: Seamlessly scale your services as you onboard new clients and users—without the hassle of hiring.
