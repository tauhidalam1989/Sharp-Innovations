Data Security Services

Protecting Your Data with Advanced Discovery, Classification, and Prevention Solutions

At Sharp Innovations, we understand that data is the most valuable asset of any organization. With the increasing threat of cyberattacks, regulatory compliance requirements, and insider threats, securing sensitive data is critical. Our Data Security Services provide a comprehensive approach to Data Discovery, Data Classification, and Data Loss Prevention (DLP) to ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of your data.

Our Core Services

  • Data Loss Prevention (DLP): To prevent unauthorized access, accidental exposure, or malicious exfiltration of sensitive data, our DLP solutions offer:
    • Endpoint DLP – Protect sensitive data on laptops, desktops, mobile devices, and removable storage.
    • Network DLP – Monitor and control data movement across enterprise networks, emails, and file transfers.
    • Cloud DLP – Secure data stored and shared across cloud platforms like Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, and SaaS applications.
    • Real-Time Policy Enforcement – Implement rules to block, quarantine, or encrypt sensitive data when policy violations occur.
    • Incident Response & Analytics – Receive alerts, audit logs, and forensic insights to detect and mitigate data breaches proactively.
  • Data Discovery:

    • Identifying and locating sensitive data across your enterprise is the first step in ensuring robust security. Our Data Discovery services provide:

    • Automated Scanning & Discovery – Identify sensitive data across structured (databases, data warehouses) and unstructured (emails, documents, cloud storage) environments.
    • Data Mapping & Inventory – Gain visibility into data flow, storage locations, and potential security vulnerabilities.
    • Cloud & On-Premises Coverage – Discover sensitive data across endpoints, cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud), and on-premises networks.
    • Compliance Readiness – Identify compliance gaps related to GDPR, HIPAA, CCPA, PCI-DSS, and other regulatory frameworks.
  • Data Classification:

    • Once data is discovered, classifying it based on sensitivity, value, and regulatory requirements is essential for effective protection. Our Data Classification services include:

    • Automated & Manual Tagging – Assign predefined or customizable classification labels such as Confidential, Internal, Public, or Restricted.
    • Integration with Security Policies – Ensure classified data is governed by role-based access controls and encryption policies.
    • AI-Driven Context Analysis – Use machine learning to identify patterns, context, and intent behind data usage.
    • Metadata & Policy-Based Classification – Apply classification based on content, file type, metadata, and business rules.
    • User Training & Awareness – Educate employees on handling classified data securely to reduce insider threats.
